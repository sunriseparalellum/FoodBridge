FOODBRIDGE USER GUIDE
=====================

1. WHAT FOODBRIDGE DOES
-----------------------
FoodBridge helps businesses share surplus food or materials with volunteers.
Businesses publish listings with a location and pickup details. Volunteers
find an available listing, claim it, and confirm pickup.

The site also contains sustainability articles and a donation tax estimate.

2. STARTING THE APPLICATION
---------------------------
Ask the project administrator to start both services. When they are running,
open:

	http://localhost:4200/

You need an account to use listings, the map, and account functions.

3. REGISTERING AND SIGNING IN
-----------------------------
1. Select Registration.
2. Enter a username, email, and password.
3. Choose a role:
	 - Entrepreneur / business: publish and manage listings.
	 - Volunteer: claim and collect listings.
4. Submit the form.
5. Select Login and enter the username and password.

Choose the role that matches your work. Listing actions are restricted by the
selected role.

4. BUSINESS WORKFLOW
--------------------
Create a listing:
1. Sign in as a business user.
2. Open S2P.
3. Select Add listing.
4. Choose a category: ready food, repurposing, or temporary storage.
5. Select Enter address and search for an address, or select a point on the
	 map.
6. Enter a title and quantity. These fields are required.
7. Optionally enter food type, description, pickup start/end, and partner
	 facility.
8. Select Publish.

Manage listings:
- Use Nearby to view open listings around the map center.
- Use My listings to view listings created by your account.
- Select Edit to change the title, quantity, or partner facility.
- Select Delete to remove your listing.

The category icons identify the listing type:
- Fork and spoon: ready food.
- Recycling: repurposing.
- Timer: temporary storage.

5. VOLUNTEER WORKFLOW
---------------------
1. Sign in as a volunteer.
2. Open S2P and review the map or listing cards.
3. Use the category tabs to filter listings.
4. Select Accept on an open listing.
5. Collect the listing during its pickup window.
6. Select Confirm pickup on the listing you accepted.

Only the volunteer who accepted a listing can confirm its pickup. A listing
moves through these statuses:
	Open -> Claimed -> Completed

6. ARTICLES
----------
Select Articles in the navigation to read published sustainability and
FoodBridge content. Articles may include a cover image, summary, full text,
and an external link.

7. TAX CALCULATOR
-----------------
Business users can select Calculator and enter:
- Donation value
- Taxable income for the period
- Whether the business is monitored as a large taxpayer

The calculator displays the maximum deductible amount, accepted amount, and
estimated corporate income tax savings. The current default rule is for
Kazakhstan. This is an estimate based on stored tax rules and does not replace
advice from an accountant or tax professional.

8. PASSWORD CHANGE
------------------
1. Sign in.
2. Select Change password.
3. Enter the current password and a new password.
4. Submit the form.

The new password must satisfy the server's Django password validation rules.

9. LOCATION AND MAP HELP
------------------------
- Address search requires an internet connection and a valid 2GIS service key.
- Map selection sets the listing coordinates where you click.
- If address lookup fails, try a more precise address or select the location
	directly on the map.
- On a phone, the navigation, forms, tabs, and map adapt to the smaller screen.

10. COMMON PROBLEMS
-------------------
Cannot log in:
- Check the username and password.
- Confirm the backend service is running.

Cannot publish a listing:
- Confirm you are signed in as a business user.
- Enter both a title and quantity.
- Select a location on the map or resolve an address.

Cannot accept a listing:
- Confirm you are signed in as a volunteer.
- The listing may already be claimed or completed.

No articles or listings appear:
- Check the selected category and view mode.
- Confirm the backend is available and the account token has not expired.
